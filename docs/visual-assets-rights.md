# Visual assets: rights and admission register

Reviewed 2026-10-23–24 JST. Scope: the free Baseball Notes Web/PWA and Android informational UI, local storage, offline cache, repository/build distribution and Pages delivery. No permission requests, contracts, payments or accounts were submitted. This is a scoped implementation decision, not a blanket determination about every legally possible use of a mark.

## How decisions are made

- A: adequate basis for the intended use. B: intended use subject to recorded conditions. C: a material issue remains unresolved. D: the proposed acquisition/republication conflicts with an express condition without permission.
- The logo table evaluates **copying and redistributing official-site logo files as app UI assets**. It does not declare club names, objective facts, all independent photographs, or every descriptive trademark use prohibited. Each independently supplied file needs a separate decision.
- Copyright licenses, trademark/endorsement implications, personality/publicity, venue restrictions and provider contracts are distinct. [Commons non-copyright restrictions](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Commons:Non-copyright_restrictions) and [identifiable people guidance](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Commons:Photographs_of_identifiable_people) explicitly separate these questions. Copyright clearance alone does not clear advertising or endorsements.
- Official download availability, a vector file, a simple design, or a Commons listing is not a general grant. Conversely, absence of an express permission is not itself proof that every factual/descriptive use is unlawful. Registration or fan-club contracts have not been silently applied to non-members.

## Jersey numbers, club identity and future admission

Only the existing verified membership/season fields may supply a jersey number. No number is inferred from a photo, old uniform, club name, draft selection or presumed present affiliation. No new roster/number facts were acquired. Historical player portraits explicitly identify the shooting year. Plain readable team names and scores provide identification when a mark is not admitted; no AI imitation is used.

New asset admission must record exact source/version, creator, license, modifications, original and optimized hashes, canonical ID + independent identity evidence, permitted screen/year/context, attribution and unresolved noncopyright constraints. The present small registry is presentation-only: it changes no canonical DB, identity mapping, source-stat contract, Facts, gates or current-player metadata. Removal is a registry/asset commit followed by the normal versioned application rebuild; no immutable statistical release is overwritten.

Original generated atmosphere assets and prompts are recorded in [editorial-ui-redesign.md](editorial-ui-redesign.md). Those scenes contain no people, official marks or real event claims.
